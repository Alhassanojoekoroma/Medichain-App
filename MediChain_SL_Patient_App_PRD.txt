1. Executive Summary
This document is the complete Product Requirements Document (PRD) for the MediChain SL Patient Native Mobile Application. It covers all 15 screens, functional requirements, technical specifications, user journeys, design guidelines, and the development roadmap.
MediChain SL is a blockchain-powered healthcare platform built specifically for Sierra Leone. The Patient App is the first product to be built and launched. It gives patients complete ownership and control over their medical records while enabling secure, consent-based sharing with verified doctors through a one-time QR code system.
The Doctor Web Application will be built in a separate phase after the Patient App is complete and validated.
1.1 Why Build the Patient App First
Patients are the primary data owners — no records can exist without patients first enrolling.
Patient adoption drives doctor adoption. A network of engaged patients creates immediate value for doctors.
The QR-based consent mechanism originates on the patient side — the doctor app is the receiver.
Validating patient UX in real-world Sierra Leone conditions de-risks the entire platform.
Faster time-to-market with a focused single-app scope.
1.2 Problem Statement
Sierra Leone's healthcare system faces critical challenges with medical record management:
Patients lose paper records when moving between facilities, leading to duplicate testing and dangerous medication errors.
No centralised system exists for verifying patient identity or medical history across hospitals.
Doctors lack access to patient records at the point of care, increasing misdiagnosis risk.
Rural patients cannot easily access specialists or follow up on care.
No audit trail exists for who has accessed a patient's records.
1.3 Solution — Patient App
The MediChain SL Patient App solves these problems by providing:
A patient-controlled, blockchain-secured digital health record system.
One-time QR code access tokens that give doctors time-limited, scoped access to patient records.
AI-powered display of structured medical data extracted from uploaded documents.
USSD fallback for patients without smartphones (*326#).
Offline-first architecture for areas with poor connectivity.
Medicine tracking, drug interaction alerts, and appointment booking in a single platform.

4.2 QR Handshake Protocol
The QR access system is the core security mechanism visible to the patient:
Patient opens QR Share screen (P-07) and selects scope (all records or a specific record).
Server generates a one-time token with a 5-minute expiry window.
QR code encodes: patient ID, session token, scope, and expiry timestamp.
Doctor scans QR code with their verified doctor app.
Server validates token, logs access on blockchain, and opens a timed session.
After 5 minutes (or when session ends), the token is burned and access is revoked.
Patient receives a real-time push notification when their QR is scanned.
4.3 Offline-First Strategy
The Patient App must function in Sierra Leone's low-connectivity environment:
Core screens (Home, Records list, Medicine Tracker) work fully offline using local SQLite.
Records are synced when connectivity is restored — background sync with conflict resolution.
QR generation requires internet (security requirement — server-signed tokens only).
Offline mode is indicated by a persistent status banner in the UI.
Last-synced timestamp is shown to the patient for transparency.

6. Screen Specifications — Phase 3: QR Access & Security
P-07: QR Share Screen ★  KEY SCREEN
Route: /records/share  |  Phase: Phase 3 — QR Access & Security
KEY SCREEN — This is the most critical screen in the Patient App
This screen is the core value proposition of MediChain SL. Everything in the product
leads to this moment: a patient generating a secure QR code and handing access to a doctor.
It must be fast, clear, and work correctly every single time.
Layout & Components
Patient identity card at top: full name, patient ID, blood type — on blue card.
Generated QR code: large (minimum 240x240 dp), centered, branded blue, MediChain SL logo watermark in center.
One-time access key code displayed below QR in monospace font (e.g., MC-7492-XKQR).
Countdown timer prominently displayed: 'Expires in 04:32' — counts down in real time, red below 60 seconds.
Scope selector: radio buttons — 'Share all records' or 'Share specific record' (with record picker modal).
Primary button: 'Generate new key' — invalidates current token and creates a new one.
Danger outline button: 'Revoke access now' — immediately burns current token on the blockchain.
Warning notice: 'Single use — expires after one scan or 5 minutes'.
Screen brightness: automatically increases to maximum for easier scanning.
Business Logic
QR generation requires active internet connection — show clear error if offline.
POST /qr/generate — server returns signed token with QR payload.
Countdown timer runs client-side but token validity enforced server-side.
When QR is scanned: push notification sent to patient immediately.
Token is single-use — once scanned, a new QR must be generated for subsequent access.
Revoke fires POST /qr/revoke/:tokenId and confirms with success animation.
Error States
No internet: 'QR generation requires internet connection. Please connect and try again.'
Token expired: timer reaches 00:00, QR grays out with 'Expired — tap to generate new key.'
Revoke failure: retry prompt with 'Could not revoke — try again.'
Acceptance Criteria
[ ] QR generated in under 2 seconds on active connection.
[ ] Countdown timer is accurate and updates every second.
[ ] Screen brightness raises to maximum on screen open; restores on screen exit.
[ ] 'Share specific record' picker shows all patient records.
[ ] Push notification received by patient when QR is scanned.
[ ] Revoke invalidates token within 1 second (verified server-side).
[ ] Offline state shows correct error — no QR shown.

7.2 Key Patient Scenarios
Scenario A — Emergency Hospital Visit
Patient arrives at hospital unconscious with no paperwork.
Doctor uses Emergency Access on the Doctor App (no patient action required).
System logs emergency access on blockchain and sends retroactive push notification to patient.
Patient sees the emergency access in P-08 with orange 'EMERGENCY ACCESS' highlight.
Scenario B — Specialist Referral (Scoped QR)
Patient opens P-07 and selects 'Share specific record' — picks GP referral letter.
Specialist scans QR and sees only that one record — no other records visible.
After 5 minutes, access expires automatically.
Patient sees the access event in P-08 Access Log.
Scenario C — USSD Fallback (Feature Phone)
Patient without a smartphone dials *326# on their feature phone.
USSD menu prompts for National ID number.
System generates a 6-digit lookup code.
Patient tells the doctor the code verbally.
Doctor enters the code manually in the Doctor App fallback field.
Access session begins as if a QR was scanned.

14. Glossary
Term
Definition
Blockchain Hash
A unique 64-character cryptographic fingerprint of a medical record, stored on-chain to prove the record has not been altered.
One-time Token
A single-use QR access code that is invalidated after first scan or after 5 minutes, whichever comes first.
USSD
Unstructured Supplementary Service Data — a protocol for feature phones accessed by dialing a short code (*326#).
AI Parser
The AI engine that reads an uploaded medical document and extracts structured medical data (diagnoses, medications, allergies).
Emergency Access
A doctor override that allows access to patient records without a QR code — requires hospital credentials and is logged on-chain.
Scope
The set of records a patient chooses to share when generating a QR code: all records, recent records, or a single record.
HPCSL
Health and Pharmaceutical Council of Sierra Leone — the medical licensing body that verifies doctors on the platform.
JWT
JSON Web Token — the secure token used to authenticate patient sessions. Expires after 24 hours.
SQLite
Local database stored on the patient's device for offline-first record caching and medicine tracking.
DPG
Digital Public Good — an open-source solution meeting the Digital Public Goods Alliance standard. MediChain SL is DPG-aligned.
