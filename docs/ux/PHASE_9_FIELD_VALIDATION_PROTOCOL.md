# Phase 9 Field Validation Protocol

Use synthetic cases with representative patients, clinicians, facility staff and Ministry analysts. Obtain research consent and do not collect real clinical content in usability recordings.

Test the critical journeys at 100%, 200% and 400% zoom; keyboard only; Windows screen reader/Chromium; iOS VoiceOver; Android TalkBack; reduced motion; high contrast; small phones; landscape; shared devices; camera unavailable; slow 2G, intermittent and offline/reconnect conditions.

For each journey record task completion, time, assistance, wrong-patient/wrong-action near misses, comprehension, recovery success and severe accessibility findings. Test both English and approved local-language content with health-literacy variation. Krio strings currently in source are engineering placeholders and require local language/content approval.

Exit requires no unresolved severe issue that can cause wrong-patient or wrong-action behavior, WCAG 2.2 AA manual review with no critical/serious finding, performance budgets under representative devices/load, understood downtime recovery and signed clinical/product/accessibility review. Automated checks alone cannot satisfy this protocol.
